About these numbers & data availability

Revenue is item price × quantity from paid, non-cancelled Trading API orders; it excludes shipping, taxes, refunds, fees, and other adjustments. Views are listing views returned by eBay Analytics for the selected period.

For a sold listing, views per sale is selected-period views divided by sold units. Time to sell runs from the original listing date to its first paid order in the selected period. Category sell-through is sold listings divided by current active listings plus listings sold in the selected period; historical ended listings are excluded because their period availability is unknown.

Promoted Listings figures come from asynchronous Marketing API reports. General campaign sales use eBay’s cost-per-sale attribution; Priority campaign sales use cost-per-click attribution. eBay may reconcile recent advertising figures for up to 72 hours.

A refresh saves the selected 7, 30, or 90-day period separately. Reauthorizing eBay is required once after Analytics and Marketing scopes are added.
