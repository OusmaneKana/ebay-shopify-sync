About these numbers & data availability

Sales cover the selected calendar period (UTC) and use paid, non-canceled orders. Revenue is transaction price × quantity before discounts, refunds, shipping, taxes, and fees; it is not profit or net payout. Currencies are displayed separately.

Views and favorites are cumulative counters returned by Etsy. For a sold single-stock listing, views per sale uses the last positive cumulative view count available at or after the sale; it remains unavailable when Etsy returns no usable counter. Time to sell runs from the original listing date to its first paid order in the selected period.

Category sell-through is sold listings divided by current active listings plus listings sold in the selected period. Historical ended listings are excluded because their period availability is unknown. Categories use Etsy’s top-level seller taxonomy.

Ratings cover available customer reviews, regardless of the selected sales period. Etsy does not provide viewer identities, search impressions, cart events, or traffic attribution through these endpoints. Favorite growth is net change, not the number of new favorite events.
